Platform and DevOps engineer with 10+ years of experience and hands-on AWS since 2016, including on-premises-to-cloud and cloud-to-cloud migrations. Tech lead of the Platform team at Trustly, specialized in Kubernetes on Amazon EKS (Karpenter, Istio with mTLS across multiple clusters and regions, ArgoCD GitOps), infrastructure as code (Terraform/OpenTofu) and observability at scale. Track record of cutting cloud cost, eliminating toil and enabling developer self-service across fintech, e-commerce, banking and retail.
